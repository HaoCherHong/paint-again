# Code of Conduct

Paint Again follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and experience level, and keep criticism about the work, not the
person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated anywhere in
the project — issues, pull requests, discussions or any other channel.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email the maintainer at
**rax333j@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer may
warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, as
described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
